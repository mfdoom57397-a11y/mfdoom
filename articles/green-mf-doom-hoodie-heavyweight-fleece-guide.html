3. Sizing Architecture & Streetwear Fit Guide

Because the 450 GSM fleece is cross-grain cut, horizontal shrinkage in the wash is completely prevented. When selecting your size:

  • True to Size: Delivers a clean, tailored streetwear drape with natural drop shoulders that sit cleanly over graphic T-shirts.
  • Size Up: For the classic 2000s baggy aesthetic, sizing up provides an oversized silhouette with roomy sleeves and stackable hem over cargo pants or skate shoes.
